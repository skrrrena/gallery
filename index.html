About the practice

Working
across everything

I'm a visual artist with a BFA in Fine Art, working across photography, ceramics, and moving image. My practice refuses to settle into a single medium — the movement between them is part of the thinking.

There's something I keep returning to: the way certain materials hold time differently. Clay remembers pressure. Photographs arrest light. Video unravels duration. I'm interested in what each medium cannot say, and what that silence reveals.

"I make things in order to understand what I'm looking at."

Currently based between studio and elsewhere. Open to residencies, collaborations, and slow conversations.
